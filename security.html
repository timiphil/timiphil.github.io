About

I'm a cybersecurity analyst focused on SOC operations and detection engineering. My day-to-day lives on the blue side — triaging alerts, validating IOCs, and investigating in Splunk and QRadar — but I spend just as much time with offensive tooling, because the fastest way to catch an attack is to understand how it's run.

I build detection tooling in C++ and Python, and I write hands-on security walkthroughs that pair each attacker step with what it leaves behind for defenders. Always learning, always shipping.
